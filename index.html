THE RESEARCH

Abstract

01

We present M5-TTS, a compact text-to-speech system supporting multilingual and Chinese multidialect synthesis, zero-shot voice cloning, speaker-specific multilingual generation, and CPU streaming. Its 50M-parameter language model, excluding the speech codec, combines a temporal backbone with a local multi-token prediction module over 16 codebooks. GRPO post-training combines global speaker-similarity and pause feedback with a token-level CER process advantage. Character-level error comparisons provide local feedback, with negative credit propagated backward within a clause and projected onto audio-token positions.

M5-TTS achieves 1.37% CER on Seed-ZH and 2.91% WER on Seed-EN, corresponding to relative error reductions of 62.2% and 35.3% over MOSS-TTS-Nano with half the language-model parameters. It also achieves lower content error rates in five of six evaluated MiniMax languages, while difficult Chinese inputs remain a limitation. On four Intel Xeon Gold 6530 cores, it achieves a real-time factor of 0.653 and a warm first-chunk latency of 225 ms. A 10-listener evaluation gives M5-TTS higher speaker-similarity and intelligibility ratings than MOSS-TTS-Nano, with a lower naturalness rating. We will release training, post-training, and inference code, together with voice-cloning and speaker-specific checkpoints, to support reproducible research on compact speech generation for on-device voice interaction.

FROM WAVEFORMS TO TOKENS

Speech tokenizer

02

A low-frame-rate, causal representation for streaming speech. M5-TTS adopts Qwen-TTS-Tokenizer-12Hz, encoding each frame into one semantic token and 15 complementary acoustic tokens.

01

Semantic + acoustic

One semantic codebook and 15 acoustic codebooks preserve complementary speech information.

02

Low frame rate

12.5 frames per second keeps the temporal sequence compact for efficient generation.

03

Causal reconstruction

No look-ahead in the encoder or decoder, enabling incremental waveform synthesis.

SMALL MODEL, STRUCTURED GENERATION

Model architecture

03

A temporal backbone Transformer models speech across frames, while a lightweight multi-token prediction (MTP) module refines the acoustic representation within each frame. Pre-training uses accent and language IDs; speaker ID is added during speaker-specific fine-tuning.

One frame, sixteen tokens. The backbone retains its attention cache across frames; the MTP cache resets for each new frame. This separates temporal modeling from within-frame acoustic refinement without expanding all 16 codebooks into separate backbone time steps.
